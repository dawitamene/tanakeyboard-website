Enkutatash (እንቁጣጣሽ)

Enkutatash is the Ethiopian New Year, on መስከረም 1. It comes as the long rainy season ends and the hills turn yellow with አደይ አበባ (Meskel daisies). The next Enkutatash is on Sunday 12 September 2027.

About Enkutatash

  • Children, especially girls, go from house to house singing አበባዬ ሆሽ and giving flowers or drawings.
  • Families gather for a holiday meal and coffee, and people wish each other a good year.
  • The date is 11 September, or 12 September in the year after an Ethiopian leap year.
