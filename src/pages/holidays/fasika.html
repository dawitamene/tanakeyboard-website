Fasika (ፋሲካ)

Fasika is Ethiopian Orthodox Easter. Its date changes every year and follows the Orthodox calculation, so it is often a week or more after Western Easter. The next Fasika is on Sunday 2 May 2027.

About Fasika

  • It ends ዐቢይ ጾም, the 55-day Lent, during which many people eat no meat, eggs or dairy.
  • ስቅለት (Siklet, Good Friday) comes two days before, and the Easter service runs late into Saturday night.
  • People greet each other with ክርስቶስ ተንሥአ እሙታን, “Christ is risen from the dead”.
