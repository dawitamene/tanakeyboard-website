About Timket

  • On the eve, ከተራ (Ketera), priests carry the tabots from each church to a pool or river in procession.
  • At dawn the water is blessed and sprinkled on the crowd, and the tabots return to their churches with singing and dancing.
  • The date is 19 January, or 20 January in the year after an Ethiopian leap year.
