How to type Amharic on Android

It takes about two minutes. You install an Amharic keyboard, turn it on once in Settings, and from then on you can write አማርኛ in WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, TikTok and every other app.

1. Install an Amharic keyboard

Install Addiyon Keyboard from Google Play. It is free and has no ads. You type Amharic with English letters, so you do not need to learn a new layout. (Other options are compared in Amharic keyboards for Android.)

2. Turn the keyboard on

  1. Open Addiyon Keyboard from your app list.
  2. Tap Enable in settings. Android opens its list of keyboards.
  3. Switch Addiyon Keyboard on, and tap OK on the notice Android shows for every new keyboard.

If you ever need to find that screen yourself, it is under Settings › System › Keyboard › On-screen keyboard on recent Android versions, or Settings › System › Languages & input › On-screen keyboard on older ones. Samsung, Tecno and Infinix put it somewhere else; see the Samsung and Tecno and Infinix guides.

3. Make it your keyboard

  1. Go back to Addiyon and tap Switch keyboard.
  2. Choose Addiyon Keyboard in the list.

Android remembers the choice. Every text box now opens with Addiyon.

4. Type Amharic

Open any chat and type the Amharic sound with English letters. As you type selam, the word appears as ሰላም, and the suggestion bar offers whole words from the Amharic dictionary. Tap a suggestion or press space to commit it.

  • selamሰላም
  • yqrtaይቅርታ
  • beTamበጣም
  • amargnaአማርኛ

Need ጠ, ጨ or ኛ? The Amharic spelling rules explain which letters need Shift.

5. Switch to English and back

Tap the globe key beside the space bar to switch between the Amharic and English layouts. English has its own suggestions, so you can write mixed Amharic and English messages without leaving the keyboard.

Extra things worth knowing

  • Ge’ez numbers: tap 123, then ፩፪, for ፩ ፪ ፫ ፲ ፻ and Amharic punctuation.
  • Voice typing: tap the microphone to dictate through your phone’s speech service.
  • Offline: typing and suggestions work without internet or mobile data.
  • AI quick edits: Fix grammar, Professional, Shorten and five more, above the keys, free each day.
