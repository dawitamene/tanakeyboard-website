How to type Amharic with an English keyboard

Amharic is easy to type with English letters once you know a handful of rules. These are the spellings used by Addiyon Keyboard and the online typing tool, which follow SERA, the common standard for writing Amharic in Latin letters.

1. A consonant plus a vowel makes one letter

Every Fidel letter is a syllable. Type the consonant, then the vowel. The vowel decides which of the seven forms you get:

VowelFormበ rowመ row
e1stbe በme መ
u2ndbu ቡmu ሙ
i3rdbi ቢmi ሚ
a4thba ባma ማ
ie5thbie ቤmie ሜ
none6thb ብm ም
o7thbo ቦmo ሞ

The two that surprise people: e is the first form, so the é in ቤት (house) is ie: biet. And the sixth form needs no vowel at all: ስም is sm, ልጅ is lj, ትምህርት is tmhrt.

2. A vowel on its own writes the አ row

When a vowel does not follow a consonant, it uses አ: a → አ, u → ኡ, i → ኢ, ie → ኤ, e → እ, o → ኦ. So endiet is እንዴት and ityoPya is ኢትዮጵያ. A capital vowel writes the ዐ row instead: Ayn → ዐይን.

3. Capitals for the sharp sounds

Amharic has ejective consonants that English does not. Use Shift for them:

LowercaseCapitalExample
t ትT ጥbeTam → በጣም
ch ችC ጭCew → ጨው
p ፕP ጵityoPya → ኢትዮጵያ
ts ጽTs ፅTseHey → ፀሐይ
h ህH ሕHzb → ሕዝብ
s ስS ሥSra → ሥራ

ቀ has its own letter, q: qen → ቀን, mesqel → መስቀል. Every other capital types the same as its lowercase letter, so a capital at the start of a name does no harm: Dawit → ዳዊት.

4. Two-letter sounds

TypeLetterExample
sh or xሽmshet → ምሸት
ch or cችljoch → ልጆች
gn or NኝseNo → ሰኞ
zhዥzhurnal → ዡርናል
khኽkhe → ኸ

Because sh, ch and gn are read as one letter, a word that really has ግ followed by ነ (like አመሰግናለሁ) cannot be spelled letter by letter yet. For now, copy it from Common Amharic phrases. When ች is followed by ሁ, add an extra h: alachhu → አላችሁ.

5. The wa forms

Add ua after a consonant for its wa form: lua → ሏ, bua → ቧ, dua → ዷ. For ቀ, ከ, ገ and ኸ, which have a full w row, type a capital W and the vowel:

  • qWaቋ
  • kWaኳ
  • gWaጓ
  • qWeቈ
  • gWiጒ
  • kWኵ

So qWanqWa is ቋንቋ (language) and gWadeN is ጓደኝ.

6. Punctuation

A full stop . types ።, a comma , types ፣ and ? types ፧. The alphabet chart lists the rest.
