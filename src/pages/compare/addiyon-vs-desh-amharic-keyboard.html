What Desh Amharic Keyboard users report

We read several hundred recent Desh Amharic Keyboard reviews on Google Play in October 2026. These are the problems that came up more than once, and how Addiyon handles each one.

Missing letters such as ኳ, ሏ and ሟ

Several reviewers say they cannot find some letters, especially the wa forms like ኳ ሏ ሟ ጓ (for example February 2024 and March 2026).

Addiyon: Addiyon types every wa form by sound: kua → ኳ, lua → ሏ, mua → ሟ, gua → ጓ.

Needs the internet

Reviewers report that conversion works poorly offline or only for short words, and the listing says offline support is still coming.

Addiyon: Addiyon’s typing and Amharic suggestions work fully offline.

No Ethiopian numerals

A reviewer notes there are no Ge’ez numerals.

Addiyon: Addiyon has a Ge’ez numerals page (፩ ፪ ፫ … ፼) and Amharic punctuation.

Cannot resize the keyboard

A reviewer asks for a way to change the keyboard size.

Addiyon: Addiyon lets you change the keyboard height in Settings.

What makes Addiyon different

  • Type by sound. amargna → አማርኛ. The whole word is re-read on every keystroke, so a later letter can still fix an earlier one.
  • Offline Amharic dictionary. Suggestions for whole words and the next word, with no data used.
  • Every Fidel letter, including the wa forms, plus Ge’ez numerals and Amharic punctuation.
  • AI quick edits for Amharic: Fix grammar, Professional, Friendly, Shorten and more, with Undo.
  • No ads, themes you can make yourself, and an adjustable keyboard height.
