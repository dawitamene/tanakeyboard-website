Addiyon vs Keyman for Amharic

Keyman is a free, open-source keyboard platform from SIL, and its GFF Amharic keyboard is one of the most used Amharic keyboards on computers. Addiyon is an Android keyboard built only for Amharic and English. (We make Addiyon.)

Choose Keyman if…

  • you want the same Amharic keyboard on your computer and your phone;
  • you need other Ethiopian languages or Ge’ez-script keyboards Keyman offers;
  • open source matters to you.

Choose Addiyon if…

  • you type mostly on an Android phone and want one app with Amharic and English together;
  • you want swipe typing, themes and AI edits for Amharic grammar and tone;
  • you want setup to take two minutes.

Our honest take: on a computer, Keyman is a great choice and we recommend it in our PC guide. On an Android phone, Addiyon is built to make everyday Amharic messaging faster.
