Choose Gboard if…

  • you type in several languages besides Amharic and English;
  • you want to speak or hand-write Amharic rather than type it;
  • you also need the same keyboard on an iPhone.

Choose Addiyon if…

  • you think of Amharic words by sound and want to type them with the English letters you know;
  • you want whole-word Amharic suggestions that work offline;
  • you want to fix grammar or change the tone of an Amharic message in one tap;
  • you write Ge’ez numbers and Ethiopic punctuation often.

Our honest take: if Amharic is a small part of your typing, Gboard is excellent and you may not need anything else. If you type Amharic every day, a keyboard built around it saves time. Try Addiyon for a week alongside Gboard; switching between them takes one tap.
