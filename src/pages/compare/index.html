Which one should you pick?

You type Amharic every day on your phone

Pick a keyboard that converts English letters and suggests whole words, so you are not hunting for letters. Addiyon and Keyman both do this. Addiyon is made only for Amharic and English on Android, has no ads and adds AI edits; Keyman also runs on computers. See Addiyon vs Keyman.

You mostly type English and sometimes Amharic

If you already use Gboard or SwiftKey, adding Amharic as a second language there means one less app. Gboard also has Amharic voice typing and handwriting. See Addiyon vs Gboard.

You prefer tapping Fidel directly

If you learned to type on a Fidel layout, HaHu offers several. Addiyon’s keys show the Fidel letter each key starts, but you always type by sound.

You need it on a computer too

Keyman works on Windows, Mac and Linux. Or use our free online Amharic typing tool, which uses the same spelling as Addiyon. More in Amharic keyboard for PC.

What makes Addiyon different

  • The whole word is re-read on every keystroke, so a later letter can still fix an earlier one, and backspace never leaves half-converted letters.
  • Suggestions from an Amharic dictionary, offline. Type bet and ቤት is offered even though the strict spelling is biet.
  • AI quick edits for Amharic: Fix grammar, Professional, Friendly, Shorten and more, free each day.
  • No ads, and typing stays on your phone.

Where others do better: Addiyon has no iPhone or computer version yet, no handwriting input, and its voice typing uses your phone’s speech service rather than its own.
