How the Ethiopian calendar works

  • Twelve months of 30 days, then ጳጉሜ with 5 days, or 6 every fourth year. 2019 E.C. is a leap year, so it ends with ጳጉሜ 6.
  • The year starts in September, on Enkutatash, መስከረም 1.
  • Years are written with ዓ.ም. (ዓመተ ምሕረት, “year of mercy”), shortened to E.C. in English.
  • Formal dates often use Ge’ez numerals, for example ጥቅምት ፬ ቀን ፳፻፲፱ ዓ.ም. Use the Ge’ez number converter for any number.

Type Ethiopian dates on your phone

With Addiyon Keyboard you type month names from English letters (meskerem → መስከረም), and the ፩፪ key on the number page gives every Ge’ez numeral. For the time of day, see the Ethiopian time converter.
