Ethiopian time converter

Pick a time to see it the way people say it in Ethiopia. The Ethiopian day starts at 6 a.m., so 8:00 a.m. is ከጠዋቱ 2 ሰዓት, “2 o’clock in the morning”.

Morning, day, evening and night

  • ከጠዋቱ, in the morning: 6 a.m. to noon
  • ከቀኑ, in the day: noon to 6 p.m.
  • ከምሽቱ, in the evening: 6 p.m. to midnight
  • ከሌሊቱ, at night: midnight to 6 a.m.

Half past is ተኩል: 8:30 a.m. is ከጠዋቱ 2 ሰዓት ተኩል. Minutes are ደቂቃ: 8:15 a.m. is ከጠዋቱ 2 ሰዓት ከ15 ደቂቃ.

Write times in Amharic on your phone

Addiyon Keyboard types these words from English letters as you go: keTewatu 2 se`at → ከጠዋቱ 2 ሰዓት. The backtick (`) gives the ዐ letters, as in ሰዓት. For dates, use the Ethiopian calendar converter.
