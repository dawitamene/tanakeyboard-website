Afaan Oromoo keyboard for Android

Addiyon Keyboard types Afaan Oromoo in Qubee with a key just for the apostrophe (hudhaa), Oromo word suggestions, next-word predictions and autocorrect. It is free, and Afaan Oromoo is a free download inside the app.

What you get

  • Qubee layout with a hudhaa key. The familiar QWERTY letters, with ' on the letter keys right after M. Write har’a, ga’e or bu’aa without opening the symbols page.
  • Oromo word suggestions. Start a word and the bar offers whole Afaan Oromoo words from the Oromo dictionary, apostrophes included.
  • Next-word predictions. After a word, the bar offers likely next words.
  • Autocorrect for Oromo. Small slips are fixed as you press space, using the Oromo word list.
  • Swipe typing. Glide across the letters to write a whole word.
  • Amharic and English too. Amharic (አማርኛ) and English are built in, and Tigrinya (ትግርኛ) is another free download. Tap the language key to switch.
  • Works offline. Once downloaded, typing and suggestions run on your phone. Your typing stays there.

How to get Afaan Oromoo

  1. Install Addiyon Keyboard from Google Play and turn it on (the app walks you through it; see setting up the keyboard).
  2. On the Which languages do you type? screen, pick Afaan Oromoo. It downloads in a few seconds (about 0.5 MB).
  3. Already set up? Open the app, go to Settings › Languages, tap Add a language, then Download next to Afaan Oromoo.
  4. In any app, tap the language key until it shows AO, and start typing.

Afaan Oromoo comes with the latest Addiyon update on Google Play, rolling out in October 2026. If you do not see it under Settings › Languages yet, update the app.

Typing Qubee on a phone

Qubee needs no special letters: double vowels (aa, ee, ii, oo, uu), double consonants and the pairs ch, dh, ny, ph, sh are typed key by key. The one mark that slows people down on other keyboards is the hudhaa, because it usually hides on the symbols page. On Addiyon it sits with the letters.
