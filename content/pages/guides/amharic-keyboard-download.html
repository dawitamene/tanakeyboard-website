Download an Amharic keyboard for Android

The safest way to get an Amharic keyboard is from Google Play. It takes about a minute, and you can type Amharic in every app afterwards.

Install and turn it on

  1. Tap Get it on Google Play above, then Install.
  2. Open Addiyon Keyboard and tap Enable in settings. Switch Addiyon on and confirm.
  3. Go back, tap Switch keyboard, and choose Addiyon Keyboard.
  4. Type selam in any app. It becomes ሰላም.

If your phone hides the setting, follow the guide for Samsung, Tecno, Infinix and itel, or any Android phone.

Why not download an APK?

A keyboard can see every word you type, including passwords, bank codes and private messages. APK files from download sites or Telegram channels are not checked and may have been changed. Apps from Google Play are scanned, signed by their developer, and update automatically.

What you get

  • English letters to Fidel as you type: amargna → አማርኛ.
  • Word suggestions for whole Amharic words and the next word.
  • Ge’ez numerals and Amharic punctuation on the number page.
  • Works offline, and what you type stays on your phone.
  • Quick edits when you want them: Fix grammar, Shorten, Professional, Friendly and more.
  • Themes to change how your keyboard looks.
