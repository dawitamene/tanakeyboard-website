How to type Amharic on WhatsApp

WhatsApp does not have an Amharic keyboard of its own; it uses the one on your phone. Set up an Amharic keyboard once, and you can write chats, statuses, captions and group names in አማርኛ.

Set up the keyboard

  1. Install Addiyon Keyboard and open it.
  2. Tap Enable in settings and switch Addiyon on.
  3. Tap Switch keyboard and choose Addiyon.

The full walkthrough, including where each phone keeps the setting, is in How to type Amharic on Android.

Write a message

Open a chat and type the sounds in English letters. Each word turns into Fidel as you go, and the bar above the keys suggests full words:

Mix Amharic and English

Tap the globe key to switch to English for a word or two, then tap it again for Amharic. Both layouts have their own suggestions, so mixed messages stay quick.

Statuses, group names and your profile

The same keyboard works everywhere in WhatsApp: status text, photo captions, group names and descriptions, and the About line on your profile.

Polish a message before sending

Wrote it in a hurry? Above Addiyon’s keys are Fix grammar, Friendly, Formal, Shorten and more. Tap one and the message in the box is rewritten in Amharic or English. Tap undo to get your own words back. It is free each day.

If something goes wrong

  • Boxes instead of letters: the phone has no Ethiopic font. Update Android and WhatsApp; every Android version since 5.0 includes one.
  • WhatsApp opens the old keyboard: make Addiyon the default from the keyboard switcher.
  • A word comes out wrong: check the spelling rules, or pick the right word from the suggestions.
