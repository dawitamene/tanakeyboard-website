How to type Amharic on Telegram

Telegram is where much of Ethiopia’s news, business and community talk happens, and most of it is in Amharic. Here is how to write Amharic messages, channel posts and captions on Android and on a computer.

On Android

  1. Install Addiyon Keyboard and open it.
  2. Tap Enable in settings, switch Addiyon on, then tap Switch keyboard and choose it.
  3. Open Telegram and type Amharic sounds with English letters: selam → ሰላም.

Need more detail? See How to type Amharic on Android.

Writing for a channel or group

  • Suggestions: the bar above the keys offers whole Amharic words and the next word, which speeds up long posts.
  • Punctuation: . types ። and , types ፣, so posts look properly Amharic without extra effort.
  • Dates and numbers: tap 123, then ፩፪, for Ge’ez numerals like ፳፻፲፱. The Ge’ez number converter helps with long ones.
  • Tidy up: tap Fix grammar to correct spelling and grammar, Professional for an announcement, or Shorten for a caption. Undo brings your text back.

On Telegram Desktop

On a computer you have two choices:

  • Write the post in the free online Amharic typing tool, press Copy Amharic, and paste it into Telegram Desktop or Telegram Web.
  • Install an Amharic keyboard for your computer. See Amharic keyboard for PC.

Usernames and links

Telegram usernames (the @name part) can only use English letters, numbers and underscores. Your display name, bio, channel title and description can all be in Amharic.
