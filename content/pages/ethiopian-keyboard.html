One keyboard for Amharic, Afaan Oromoo and Tigrinya

Addiyon Keyboard is a free Android keyboard for the languages of Ethiopia and Eritrea. Amharic and English are built in, and Afaan Oromoo and Tigrinya are free downloads. Each language has its own layout and its own word suggestions.

How to add a language

  1. Install Addiyon Keyboard from Google Play and turn it on.
  2. On the Which languages do you type? screen, pick the ones you want.
  3. To change them later, open the app and go to Settings › Languages. Tap Add a language, then Download. Drag languages to change their order, or remove the ones you do not use.
  4. While typing, tap the language key to switch.

Afaan Oromoo and Tigrinya come with the latest Addiyon update on Google Play, rolling out in October 2026. If you do not see them under Settings › Languages yet, update the app.

Same keyboard, every language

  • Ge’ez numbers and punctuation (፩ ፪ ፫ ። ፣) for Amharic and Tigrinya.
  • Offline: once a language is on your phone, typing and suggestions need no connection.
  • Private: your typing stays on your phone, and nothing is learned in password fields. See the privacy policy.
  • Your theme stays the same whichever language you type.
  • AI quick edits such as Fix grammar and Shorten, free each day, when you want them.

Free tools for the Ge’ez script

On a computer? Type Amharic online or type Tigrinya online with the same keys and copy the text, look up any letter in the Ge’ez alphabet chart, or convert numbers to Ge’ez.
