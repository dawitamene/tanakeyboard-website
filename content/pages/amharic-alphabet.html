Amharic alphabet chart

Every letter of the Amharic alphabet (ፊደል) in all seven forms. The small text under each letter is what you type on an English keyboard to get it. Tap any letter to copy it.

How to read the chart

Each row is one consonant, and each column is a vowel. Amharic letters are syllables: the base shape is the consonant, and small changes to it (a stroke, a ring, a shorter leg) add the vowel. That is why በ, ቡ, ቢ, ባ, ቤ, ብ and ቦ all look alike.

The sixth column (ሳድስ) is the consonant with a short ə sound or no vowel at all. It is the most common form in everyday writing: ስም (name), ልጅ (child), ፍቅር (love). To type it, type the consonant alone: sm, lj, fqr.

The last column is the wa form, which adds a w sound: ሏ, ቧ, ጓ. Type the consonant and ua. Letters like ቀ, ከ and ገ also have a full w row (ቈ ቊ ቋ ቌ ቍ); type a capital W before the vowel: qWa → ቋ, gWadeN → ጓደኝ.

Amharic punctuation

On the typing tool and in Addiyon Keyboard, . types ።, , types ፣ and ? types ፧. The rest are on the keyboard’s symbols page. Ethiopic numbers have their own chart.
