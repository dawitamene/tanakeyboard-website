What Fyn Geez users report

We read several hundred recent Fyn Geez reviews on Google Play in October 2026. These are the problems that came up more than once, and how Addiyon handles each one.

Ads while typing

Reviewers describe ads appearing in the middle of typing and having to wait several seconds to continue (for example October 2022 and July 2024).

Addiyon: Addiyon has no ads, so nothing interrupts a message.

Bottom keys hidden on newer phones

Since mid-2026, reviewers on Pixel, HiOS and other phones report the space bar and bottom keys sitting under the navigation bar.

Addiyon: Addiyon is actively maintained for current Android versions (latest update 9 October 2026) and lets you change the keyboard height in Settings.

Weak suggestions and spell-check

Several reviewers say word suggestions are missing or wrong.

Addiyon: Addiyon suggests whole Amharic words and the next word from an offline dictionary, and Fix grammar corrects a whole message.

Alphabetical Fidel layout is slow

Some reviewers find hunting through a Fidel grid slow.

Addiyon: With Addiyon you type the sound in English letters: selam → ሰላም.

What makes Addiyon different

  • Type by sound. amargna → አማርኛ. The whole word is re-read on every keystroke, so a later letter can still fix an earlier one.
  • Offline Amharic dictionary. Suggestions for whole words and the next word, with no data used.
  • Every Fidel letter, including the wa forms, plus Ge’ez numerals and Amharic punctuation.
  • AI quick edits for Amharic: Fix grammar, Professional, Friendly, Shorten and more, with Undo.
  • No ads, themes you can make yourself, and an adjustable keyboard height.
