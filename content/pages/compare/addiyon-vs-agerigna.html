What Agerigna users report

We read several hundred recent Agerigna reviews on Google Play in October 2026. These are the problems that came up more than once, and how Addiyon handles each one.

“Keeps stopping” after updates

From September 2025, many reviewers, often on recent Samsung phones, report the keyboard closing by itself after an update.

Addiyon: Addiyon is actively maintained for current Android versions; its latest update on Google Play was on 9 October 2026.

Keyboard covers the text box

Since late 2025, reviewers report the keyboard covering the message box in WhatsApp, Viber and Telegram.

Addiyon: Addiyon sits above the text box like your phone’s own keyboard, and you can change its height in Settings.

Wrong autocorrect

Some reviewers say autocorrect and suggestions change words wrongly.

Addiyon: Addiyon re-reads the whole word on every keystroke and suggests from an Amharic dictionary; your own spelling always stays one tap away.

Missing letters such as ሏ and ሟ

A few reviewers could not find some wa forms.

Addiyon: Addiyon types them by sound: lua → ሏ, mua → ሟ.

What makes Addiyon different

  • Type by sound. amargna → አማርኛ. The whole word is re-read on every keystroke, so a later letter can still fix an earlier one.
  • Offline Amharic dictionary. Suggestions for whole words and the next word, with no data used.
  • Every Fidel letter, including the wa forms, plus Ge’ez numerals and Amharic punctuation.
  • AI quick edits for Amharic: Fix grammar, Professional, Friendly, Shorten and more, with Undo.
  • No ads, themes you can make yourself, and an adjustable keyboard height.
