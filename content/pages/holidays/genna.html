Genna (ገና)

Genna is Ethiopian Orthodox Christmas, celebrated on 7 January. Many people keep a 43-day fast before it. The next Genna is on Thursday 7 January 2027.

About Genna

  • Church services run through the night before Genna, with worshippers dressed in white ነጠላ (netela).
  • The fast ends after the service, and families share doro wat and other holiday food.
  • In the Ethiopian calendar it falls on ታኅሣሥ 29, or ታኅሣሥ 28 in the year after a leap year, so the Gregorian date stays 7 January.
