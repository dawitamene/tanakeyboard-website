How Ge’ez numbers work

There are 20 numerals: one sign for each of 1 to 9 (፩–፱), one for each ten from 10 to 90 (፲–፺), one for hundred (፻) and one for ten thousand (፼). There is no zero.

To write a number, split it into pairs of digits from the right. Write each pair with a tens sign and a ones sign, and put ፻ or ፼ between the pairs:

NumberRead asGe’ez
2520 + 5፳፭
3653 × 100 + 65፫፻፷፭
1,98419 × 100 + 84፲፱፻፹፬
2,01920 × 100 + 19፳፻፲፱
10,00110,000 + 1፼፩
1,000,000100 × 10,000፻፼

A leading one is dropped before ፻ and ፼: 100 is ፻, not ፩፻.

Where they are used

Ge’ez numerals appear in Ethiopian calendar dates, church books, chapter and verse numbers, page numbers in traditional books, and on formal documents and invitations. For prices and phone numbers, everyday Amharic writing uses the ordinary digits 0–9.
