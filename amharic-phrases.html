Common questions.

How do you say hello in Amharic?

ሰላም (selam). It also means peace, and works at any time of day.

How do you say thank you in Amharic?

አመሰግናለሁ (ameseginalehu). To thank someone warmly, add በጣም (betam, very): በጣም አመሰግናለሁ.

Why are there different phrases for men and women?

Amharic changes the word ending for the person you are talking to. ነህ is for a man, ነሽ for a woman, and ነዎት is the polite form for elders or people you do not know.
