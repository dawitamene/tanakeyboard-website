Common questions.

What is Addiyon Keyboard?

An Android keyboard with Latin-to-Fidel transliteration, autocomplete, Ethiopic numerals, punctuation, and English QWERTY.

How do I type Amharic from Latin letters?

Type a sound like selam. Preview ሰላም, then tap to commit it.

Does it support Ge’ez numbers?

Yes: ፩ ፪ ፫ ፲ ፻ ፼, plus Amharic punctuation and special characters.

Is Addiyon Keyboard private?

Yes. Transliteration and suggestions run on your device. Your text is not sent to Addiyon.

Can I use it in other apps?

Yes. As a system keyboard, Addiyon works anywhere Android lets you type.

Is this the same as an online typing tool?

No. Unlike a website tool, Addiyon works directly across Android apps, even offline.
