Common questions.

What is Addiyon Keyboard?

A free Android keyboard for typing Amharic in the Ge’ez (Fidel) script. It turns Latin letters into Fidel as you type, and includes Ethiopic numerals, Amharic punctuation, offline suggestions, swipe typing and an English QWERTY layout.

How do I type Amharic with English letters?

Type the sound in Latin letters, like selam, and Addiyon shows ሰላም as you go. Tap a suggestion or press space to commit it. The spelling rules cover the letters that need Shift, like T for ጠ.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Typing, transliteration and suggestions run on your phone with no connection. Only the optional AI quick edits need the internet, and only when you tap one.

Does it support Ge’ez numbers?

Yes: ፩ ፪ ፫ ፲ ፻ ፼, plus Amharic punctuation such as ። ፣ ፤ ፧. You can also convert any number to Ge’ez online.

Is Addiyon Keyboard private?

Yes. Typing, transliteration and suggestions stay on your device. Text is sent only when you tap an AI edit, and only the text being edited. Nothing is learned in password fields.

Does it work in WhatsApp, Telegram and Facebook?

Yes. As a system keyboard, Addiyon works anywhere Android lets you type: chats, social apps, search, notes and documents.

Do I need an account?

Not to type. You sign in with Google or an email code only the first time you use an AI edit.

Is the AI free?

Yes, with a daily allowance that resets each day. Where Google Play offers it, Addiyon Pro gives about 10,000 AI edits a month and the Pro themes.

Is there an iPhone or computer version?

Not yet. Addiyon Keyboard is for Android. On a computer you can use the free online Amharic typing tool and copy the text.
