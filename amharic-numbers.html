How Amharic numbers work

  • 1 to 10 each have their own word, from አንድ to አስር.
  • 11 to 19 are አስራ plus the unit: አስራ ሁለት is 12.
  • Tens have their own words (ሃያ 20, ሰላሳ 30 …), followed by the unit: ሃያ አምስት is 25.
  • Bigger numbers use መቶ (hundred), ሺህ (thousand) and ሚሊዮን (million): ሶስት መቶ is 300.

Common questions.

How do you count to ten in Amharic?

አንድ፣ ሁለት፣ ሶስት፣ አራት፣ አምስት፣ ስድስት፣ ሰባት፣ ስምንት፣ ዘጠኝ፣ አስር.

How are numbers from 11 to 19 formed?

Put አስራ (ten-and) before the unit: 11 is አስራ አንድ, 15 is አስራ አምስት.

How do you say 2019 in Amharic?

ሁለት ሺህ አስራ ዘጠኝ: two thousand, ten-and-nine. In Ge’ez numerals it is ፳፻፲፱.

Why do I sometimes see ሦስት or አሥር?

Those are older spellings with letters that now sound the same. ሦስት and ሶስት are both three; አሥር and አስር are both ten. Sixty is also written ስድሳ.
