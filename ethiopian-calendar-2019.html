Reading the calendar

  • Weeks start on Sunday (እሑድ), as on Ethiopian wall calendars.
  • Each month has 30 days. ጳጉሜ has 6 days this year because 2019 is a leap year.
  • To convert a single date, use the Ethiopian calendar converter. For the time of day, see Ethiopian time.

Common questions.

When does 2019 E.C. start and end?

It starts on መስከረም 1, Friday 11 September 2026, and ends on ጳጉሜ 6, Saturday 11 September 2027. 2020 E.C. begins on 12 September 2027.

Is 2019 E.C. a leap year?

Yes. 2019 is a leap year (ዘመነ ሉቃስ), so ጳጉሜ has 6 days instead of 5.

Why are Fasika and Eid not marked?

Fasika (Easter), Siklet, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha and Mawlid move every year and depend on church calculation or the moon, so check the official announcement for their dates.

Can I print this calendar?

Yes. Use your browser’s Print command; the page hides the menus and prints the months only.
