Addiyon is a free Amharic keyboard for Android with no ads. Type Amharic (አማርኛ) with the English letters you already know: selam becomes ሰላም as you type. Every Ge'ez (Fidel) letter, Ge'ez numbers (፩ ፪ ፫) and Ethiopic punctuation (። ፣) are built in, and an Amharic dictionary suggests whole words, offline.

When you want help, AI quick edits above the keys fix grammar, make a message more professional or friendly, or shorten it, in Amharic or English.
