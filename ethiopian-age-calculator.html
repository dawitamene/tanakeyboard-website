How Ethiopian birthdays work

Your Ethiopian birthday is the same Ethiopian date every year, so its Gregorian date can move by a day after a leap year. Someone born on መስከረም 1 celebrates on 11 September in most years and 12 September in others.

To change any single date between calendars, use the Ethiopian calendar converter. To see the whole year, open the 2019 E.C. calendar.

Common questions.

Is my age different in the Ethiopian calendar?

Usually not. You turn a year older on your Ethiopian birthday instead of your Gregorian one, and the two fall on the same day for most of the year. Only your birth year looks different, because Ethiopian years are 7 or 8 behind.

How do I find my birth date in the Ethiopian calendar?

Enter your Gregorian birth date above. The calculator shows it in E.C., for example 1 January 2000 is ታኅሣሥ 22, 1992 E.C.

What if I was born in Pagume?

ጳጉሜ 6 only exists in leap years. In other years the calculator uses ጳጉሜ 5 as your birthday.
