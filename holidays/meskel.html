Meskel (መስቀል)

Meskel, on መስከረም 17, celebrates the finding of the True Cross. UNESCO lists the celebration as Intangible Cultural Heritage. The next Meskel is on Tuesday 28 September 2027.

About Meskel

  • On the eve, መስከረም 16, crowds light the ደመራ (Demera), a tall bonfire decorated with Meskel daisies.
  • The largest celebration is in Meskel Square in Addis Ababa.
  • The date is 27 September, or 28 September in the year after an Ethiopian leap year.

Common questions.

When is Meskel 2027?

Tuesday 28 September 2027, which is መስከረም 17, 2020 in the Ethiopian calendar.

How do you say happy Meskel in Amharic?

መልካም የመስቀል በዓል. To wish everyone a good holiday, say እንኳን አደረሳችሁ; the usual reply is እንኳን አብሮ አደረሰን.
