National days fixed to the Gregorian calendar include Adwa Victory Day (2 March), Labour Day (1 May), Patriots’ Victory Day (5 May) and Derg Downfall Day (28 May). Muslim holidays follow the moon and are announced each year. The 2019 E.C. calendar marks the fixed ones.
