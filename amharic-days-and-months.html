Common questions.

What are the days of the week in Amharic?

እሑድ (Sunday), ሰኞ (Monday), ማክሰኞ (Tuesday), ረቡዕ (Wednesday), ሐሙስ (Thursday), ዓርብ (Friday) and ቅዳሜ (Saturday).

How many months are there in Amharic?

Thirteen, because the Ethiopian calendar has twelve 30-day months and a short thirteenth month, ጳጉሜ.

Why are some month names spelled two ways?

Older writing uses letters that sound the same today, so ኅዳር and ህዳር, or ታኅሣሥ and ታህሳስ, are the same month. Both spellings are understood.
